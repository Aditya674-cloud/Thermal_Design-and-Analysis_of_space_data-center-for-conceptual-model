7.1 Ansys model preparation 
The developed CAD geometry designed in SOLIDWORKS was imported into ANSYS for thermal analysis.
The model consisted of the computing encloser, thermal transport components specifically liquid
cooling system, radiator plated. Geometrical simplifications were applied where necessary to reduce
unnecessary computational complexity while retaining the major thermal paths of the proposed
configuration.

7.6 Solver and solution setup
A steady-state thermal analysis was performed using ANSYS to 
determine the temperature distribution and heat transfer behavior of the proposed thermal
management system under the defined operating conditions. The model considered heat condition
through the solid components together with thermal radiation from the designed radiator surface
to the defined radiative environment.
The thermal design model was solved using the material properties, heat generation,
condition, and radiation boundary. The radiator emissivity and effective radiative sink 
temperature were defined consistently with the analytical model. The solution was evaluated 
using temperature distribution and total heat flux as the primary thermal outputs. These results 
were subsequently used to assess the thermal performance of the proposed configuration and to 
compare the numerical results with the analytical calculations.
