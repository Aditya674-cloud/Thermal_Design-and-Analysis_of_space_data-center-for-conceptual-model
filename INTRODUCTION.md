# What is Data Center ?
## According to WIKIPEDIA:-

A data center is a physical room, building, or facility for the purpose of the storage, management,
and dissemination of data and information, including computer systems, and associated components, housing IT infrastructure, and training artificial intelligence. 
The Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 defines a data center as "any facility that primarily contains electronic equipment used to process, store, and transmit digital information. 
This includes "a free-standing structure" or "a facility within a larger structure, that uses environmental control equipment to maintain the proper conditions for the operation of electronic
equipment. According to IBM, data centers date back to the 1940s, with the U.S. military's Electrical Numerical Integrator and Computer (ENIAC) as an early example.


## Why Space Data Center ? [STARCLOUD][https://www.starcloud.com/starcloud-4]

Orbital data centers offer several fundamental benefits compared to their terrestrial counterparts, especially
when scaled to GW sizes. Significant operational cost savings can be achieved by using inexpensive solar
energy without the limitations of terrestrial solar farms discussed below. Orbital data centers can leverage
lower cooling costs using passive radiative cooling in space to directly achieve low coolant temperatures.
Perhaps most importantly, they can be scaled almost indefinitely without the physical or permitting constraints
faced on Earth, using modularity to deploy them rapidly. All of this will have a net benefit on the environment -
a recent study by the European Commission concluded that orbital data centers will significantly reduce
greenhouse gas emissions from grid electricity and eliminate fresh water usage for cooling.
