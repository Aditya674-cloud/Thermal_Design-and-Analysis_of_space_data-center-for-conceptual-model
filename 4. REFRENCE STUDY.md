# Model Design 

Here i designed an Refrence model of Star Cloud data center in *SOLIDWORKS* design software.

The design made here is not 100% according to STARCLOUD but in this project we are trying to understand heat flow from Data center box to Radiator.

The Solar panels are not considered.
